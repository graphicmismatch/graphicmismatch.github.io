OpenFocus Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 16th November 2026

OpenFocus is a free, open-source app blocker, focus timer and habit tracker for Android, made by GraphicMismatch. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device and what happens to it. In short: everything stays on your phone.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. OpenFocus has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following on your phone only, so it can work:

  • Your habits, goals, focus sessions and their history
  • Your hero's name, level, coins, items, achievements and quest progress
  • The apps you chose to block, your schedules, limits and settings

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

If you have turned on Android's own backup, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot access it.

3. Accessibility Service

OpenFocus uses Android's AccessibilityService API for its core feature: blocking the apps and short-video feeds (such as YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels) that you choose. The app shows a disclosure and asks for your consent before you turn the service on.

  • It learns which app is currently open, so it can show the pause screen over apps you blocked.
  • Only while a short-video app you enabled is open, it reads the identifiers of on-screen elements to recognise a short-video feed and leave it.
  • It never reads text you type, passwords or messages, never records your screen, and never sends anything off your device.

You can turn the service off at any time in Settings > Accessibility > OpenFocus Shield.

4. Other Permissions

  • Usage access (optional): to measure how long you use blocked apps each day, for daily time limits. This stays on your device.
  • Notifications: for focus timer countdowns and habit reminders.
  • Installed apps: the app lists your launchable apps so you can pick which ones to block. The list is not sent anywhere.

5. Children's Privacy

OpenFocus does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

6. Open Source

OpenFocus is open source, so you can check how it handles your data: github.com/OpenFocusHQ/OpenFocus

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
